Chicago after dark

Chicago’s taxis are a downtown-and-airport business: a few square miles produce most of the rides, and the airports bring in a big share of the money.

We mapped every taxi trip in the city in June 2024 — 592,941 of them. Each glowing pillar is a place where riders were picked up; the taller it is, the more trips started there.

Scroll ↓
Finding 1 · Where rides start

Three neighborhoods, most of the rides

62%of all pickups, from three of 77 community areas

The lit areas — Near North Side, O’Hare and the Loop — are the city’s taxi economy. Everything in grey is the rest of Chicago.

The 38 least-served neighborhoods together account for just 2.8% of pickups: the typical neighborhood sees about 30 taxi pickups a day.

Finding 2 · Where the money is

The airports: a quarter of the trips, far more of the fares

24%of trips start at O’Hare or Midway
41%of all fares

An airport ride is a long ride: a median 15.3 miles and $42, against 1.9 miles and $11 for every other trip. That’s the second hump in the distance chart.

Finding 3 · Which way they go

A taxi out of the airport — but not to it

141kpickups at the airports
48kdrop-offs there

At each airport, the amber pillar is rides that started there; the cyan one beside it is rides that ended there. For every three riders who took a taxi from the airport, about one took a taxi to it.

This data shows the imbalance, not its cause: how departing travellers get to the airport isn’t in it.
