The Restless Year

The Earth’s surface is broken into plates that grind past, pull apart and dive beneath one another, and nearly every earthquake happens where they meet. This is every earthquake of magnitude 4.5 or more recorded in 2024 — large enough to be measured worldwide — placed where it began. Each step up in magnitude releases about 32 times more energy, and depth tells you what kind of boundary you are looking at: most quakes start in the top 70 km, but some begin hundreds of kilometres down, inside plates that are sinking into the mantle.
